1. System Overview & Access

1.1 The Public Showcase

The public URL is your luxury digital storefront. It is optimized for performance, mobile responsiveness, and high-end visual aesthetics.

2. Dynamic Branding & Settings

The Settings Tab in the Admin Dashboard is the central nervous system for your brand's digital presence. Changes made here reflect instantly across the entire platform.

2.1 Logos & Hero Banners

  • Cinematic Hero Carousel: The homepage features a massive auto-advancing carousel with 4 image slots (Slide 1 - Slide 4).
  • Adding/Removing: Click UPLOAD NEW to select an image, or REMOVE to clear a slot. The carousel automatically skips empty slots seamlessly.
  • Saving: You MUST click the large SAVE ASSET button to lock your changes into the database.

2.2 Company Info & Social Telemetry

  • Update your Mission Statement, Company Title, Physical Address, and Phone Numbers directly from the dashboard.
  • Social Media Links: Manage links to Telegram, Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn. Leaving a URL blank will automatically hide that specific social icon from the website footer.

3. Inventory Control

3.1 Managing Categories

The system uses a hierarchical structure to keep the catalog luxurious and organized.

  • Parent Categories: Top-level classifications (e.g., "Solar Panels") that appear in the left sidebar.
  • Sub-Categories: Appear as sleek, clickable pill-buttons at the top of the catalog when a user selects a Parent Category. Create these by assigning a "Parent Category" during creation.

3.2 Managing Products

  • Technical Specifications: Specify exact engineering details like Voltage, Capacity, Weight, and Power Output.
  • Stock Status: Flag items as In Stock, Low Stock, Preorder, or Sold Out. Sold Out items will be visibly greyed out on the public showcase.
  • Featured Showcase: Enabling the "Featured" star pushes a product to the rotating "Featured Drops" display on the homepage.

4. The FR-2 Solar Sizing Engine

4.1 Client Operations

  • Mode 1 (Appliance Checklist): Clients select standard household appliances, adjust quantities, and set their estimated daily runtime hours.
  • Mode 2 (Direct kW Input): Advanced clients can directly drag sliders for Peak Load (kW) and Daily Energy Demand (kWh).

4.2 Intelligent Output & Lead Generation

The engine mathematically calculates the required Inverter size and Battery capacity, applying standard efficiency losses.

  • System Tiers: It recommends 3 matched system packages (Essential Backup, Hybrid Kit, Master Kit).
  • One-Click Inquiry: Clients can click "Send Inquiry" to automatically format their exact load profile into a message routed directly to your WhatsApp or Telegram.

Professional PDF Reporting: The calculator is highly optimized for offline engineering reports. If a client prints the page (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P), the system aggressively hides website navigation and branding clutter, generating a clean, professional, white-label PDF engineering report.
